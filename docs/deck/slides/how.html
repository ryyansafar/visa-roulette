HOW IT WORKS

Scan. Throw. Land.

STEP 1

Scan

Show a passport and a flight ticket to the camera. OCR reads the passport, the IATA barcode gives the flight, and a checklist ticks live.

STEP 2

Throw

Throw a paper plane fitted with an ESP32 and an IMU. A webcam flick or a single switch works too. The globe slows and lands.

STEP 3

Land

Get the visa verdict with its source, the documents to carry, destination highlights, and a QR trip card for your phone.
