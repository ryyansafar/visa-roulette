Q AND A PREP. Full answers, say them in this order of confidence. 1. Is the AI deciding visas? No. Vision and OCR only read the document. A deterministic rules engine reads JSON rule files and produces the verdict, with a source link and an as-of date. A local LLM may rephrase an explanation in two plain sentences; if it says anything the rules do not contain, a template replaces it. A destination with no rule is reported as unknown, never guessed. 2. How accurate is the rules data? These are demo rules: visa_rules.json has as_of 2026-10-10 and verified false, and the UI says so. They are curated from public information with a source URL per rule. They are not legal advice. The design makes verification a data task: check each rule, flip its verified flag. That is our first next step. 3. What if OCR fails? The MRZ has check digits, so a bad read is caught rather than trusted, and the booth shows a retake hint. If the room is bad, tap a sample traveller: the sample passports are known to the system, so the flow never dead-ends. Arbitrary photo scans are a local-booth feature; the hosted demo uses sample travellers only. 4. Does it work offline? The core flow is offline-first: local Tesseract and zxing, local rules, a local Ollama model with a template fallback, local faster-whisper voice, and a QR hand-off served from the laptop over its LAN. The hosted demo needs internet by nature and has no hardware. 5. Do you handle real passports? What about privacy? The booth is built for sample or fake passports. We say so on screen. Data stays on the laptop for the local flow; we do not send passport data to a cloud model. Do not ask judges to scan real documents. 6. What if the throw is unreliable or the hardware dies? The detector rejects ambiguous throws with a reason instead of guessing (no_rest, too_weak, mount_angle, straight_up). Fallbacks feed the same flight model: webcam flick, single-switch Space key, and the recorded hardware video. BONUS (if asked): Who built what? Ryyan Safar: backend, rules, hardware, integration.
